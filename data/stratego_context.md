# Strategist Cockpit - Stratego Knowledge Base

## About the Strategist Role

The Data & AI Strategist is a trusted advisor who helps Databricks grow, solve hard customer problems, and elevate the pitch. The strategist works across multiple accounts (customers) in a region, translating business vision into data & AI programs and organizational change.

### Goal
Databricks as the DEFAULT Data & AI Platform, with focus on:
1. Unity Catalog adoption
2. Dominating DWH (Data Warehouse)
3. Lakeflow ETL adoption
4. Leading with Industry solutions
5. Winning Enterprise AI deals

### Activity Areas (Strategist Canvas)

**Thought Leadership (Customer Engagements 50% + Evangelism 10%)**
- C-level vision setting: Strategic conversations with CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, CFOs
- Data & AI Strategy: Design and review of data and AI strategies ("missions")
- Strategic Hunting: Bid development and pipeline generation
- Elevate the pitch: Keynotes, presentations, and thought leadership content
- Targeted customer engagements: Focused workshops and advisory sessions
- Measuring Success: KPIs, impact frameworks, and value tracking
- Champion Building: Establishing cadences with key stakeholders
- Focused account planning: Territory and account strategy sessions
- Events: Industry events, conferences, DAIS, DAIWT
- Market Scouting: Trends research and competitive intelligence
- Community Seeding: Building and growing data communities

**Coaching & Mentoring**
- Individual coaching for team members, customers, and partners

**Customer Mobilization**
- Adoption Frameworks: Overcoming inertia, accelerating consumption
- MVP Fast Track programs, AI Speedboats, innovation sessions

**Initiatives (20%)**
- Strategist Role: Developing the role, organization, and metrics
- Strategy CoP: Community of Practice for strategy
- Reusable Strategy Assets: Templates, playbooks, frameworks

**Admin & Research**
- Strategy research: Points of view, whitepapers, industry analysis

### Engagement Types
- **Focus**: Multi-quarter strategic advisory engagement (usually 3-12 months), indicated by "Strategic Advisor" flag in Salesforce at account level
- **One-off**: Single-quarter exercise, reflected as ASQ (Approval Request) in Salesforce
- **Customer Event**: Keynotes, panels, or conference appearances
- **Tbc**: To be confirmed engagements

### Engagement Status
- **Completed**: Successfully concluded
- **Ongoing**: Currently active
- **Abandoned**: Did not proceed (customer declined, scheduling issues, etc.)
- **Not started**: Planned but not yet kicked off
- **On hold**: Temporarily paused

### Financial Year
FY starts in February. For example:
- FY25 = February 2024 - January 2025
- FY26 = February 2025 - January 2026
- FY27 = February 2026 - January 2027

### Key Metrics (Impact)
**Customer Engagements:**
- Jointly developed data & AI programs
